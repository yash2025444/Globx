Acoustic Solutions

GLOB-X offers advanced acoustic solutions designed to reduce noise, enhance sound clarity, and improve acoustic comfort across commercial, industrial, and residential spaces.

Acoustic Flooring

Glob-X Acoustic Flooring are designed to attenuate impact and airborne noise at the foundation level. Constructed using high-density, resilient layers and recycled composite materials, they reduce vibration transfer while improving underfoot stability and comfort. These systems are ideal for high-traffic environments where acoustic control, durability, and longevity are non-negotiable — delivering consistent performance without altering architectural intent. Key Attributes:
• High impact sound reduction
• Vibration damping technology
• Long-term structural resilience
• Suitable for commercial and residential use

Acoustic Wall Panels

Glob-X Wall Panels transform walls into acoustically active surfaces. Designed to absorb excess reverberation while enhancing spatial aesthetics, each panel balances material performance with refined finishes and clean geometry. Engineered for clarity and comfort, these panels improve speech intelligibility and acoustic balance in offices, auditoriums, hospitality spaces, and public interiors — without visual disruption.

Ceiling Baffles

Glob-X Ceiling Baffles are precision-engineered for large, open environments where traditional acoustic treatments fall short. Suspended vertically to intercept sound from multiple directions, they deliver exceptional absorption while introducing rhythm, depth, and movement overhead. These systems offer designers complete creative freedom while maintaining high acoustic performance — making them ideal for offices, atriums, libraries, and public spaces. Key Attributes
• Multi-directional sound absorption
• Lightweight, suspended architecture
• Visual depth and spatial flow
• High-performance in open volumes

Fiberglass Insulation

Glob-X Fibreglass Insulation delivers superior acoustic and thermal performance within walls, ceilings, and partitions. Engineered for consistency and reliability, it provides effective noise control while supporting fire safety and energy efficiency standards. Designed to perform quietly in the background, it forms the core of high-performance building envelopes. Key Attributes
• High NRC and thermal efficiency
• Lightweight and fire-safe
• Long-lasting acoustic stability
• Ideal for concealed applications
